Proclaim Version 4.7

4.7.0.0203 (Windows only) - Wednesday, November 13, 2024

  • Ensure OSB items advance and display correctly with multiple displays.

4.7.0.0202 - Monday, November 11, 2024

New

  • Add support for output display masks. Omit parts of your slide output to account for objects in projection environments or unconventional screen shapes. Learn more
  • Improve Announcement V2 and renamed as Slide Canvas. The Slide Canvas item gives you a blank slate without any text boxes or fields and a more intuitive editor. Let us know what you think!

Improved

  • Show warning when chord transposition fails for some bracketed sections.
  • Handle “x2” song order notation on Planning Center service imports.
  • Improve visibility of slide arrows in Multi-Preview mode.

Fixed

  • Ensure Proclaim remembers the last selected recording device.
  • Clarify use of Section Headers and Verse Numbers on Bible items. Section headers may not be used without verse numbers.
  • Fix unexpected rendering of some Bible passages in OSB.
  • Avoid indentation with chord-space combinations that start a slide.
  • Prevent early ellipses for service item titles in the Order of Service. (Windows only)
  • Fix issue moving multi-selected foreground images.
  • Prevent crash creating presentation team with certain characters (“%”, etc) in the name.
  • Stop showing On Screen annotations when we shouldn't.
  • Prevent crash when signing out after starting an upload.
  • Allow deletion of Paperless Hymnal imports.
  • Clarify selectability of buttons during song lyric import.
