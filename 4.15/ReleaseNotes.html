Proclaim Version 4.15

Monday, November 11 2025 - Proclaim 4.15.0.0167

New

  • Support 4K media uploads.
  • Add QR codes to the slide editor. Learn more.
  • Add List view to media browser.

Improved

  • Show Open presentation window after switching teams.
  • Improve full and current slide preview placeholder in confidence monitor.
  • Add virtual screen tab overflow menu.
  • Re-render Service Builder bible media when passage is updated.
  • Increase virtual screen count limit from 4 to 8.
  • Don't show re-uploaded media multiple times in the Media Browser.

Bug Fixes

  • Prevent playback issues after updating sermon recordings (Mac only).
  • Fix Next slide preview with Lyric Scrolling transition selected.
  • Restore ability to add empty lines to lyric slides with shift \+ enter (Mac only).
  • Fix occasional failure importing Keynote presentations.
  • Sort hymns by value instead of first digit.
  • Wait for "show updated" to be clicked before re-rendering Confidence and Alt Content On Air.
  • Prevent background audio from stopping when advancing slide.
  • Prevent lyrics with shapes from overlapping when Lyric Scrolling is enabled.
  • Make sure background audio resumes after video.
  • Don't show empty lyrics field on Song title slide.
  • Prevent text box field from losing focus during Warm-up slide updates.
  • Keep confidence clocks and timers from disappearing when a Blank quick screen is shown.
