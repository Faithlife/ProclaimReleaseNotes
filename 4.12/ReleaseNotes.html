  • Show the correct first frame when video length is modified.

  • Prevent duplicate keys in song key dropdown.
  • Ensure screen identifier overlay is scaled correctly when "Identify displays" is clicked (Windows only).
  • Hide slide settings in the confidence monitor (Windows only).
  • Prevent overlapping message box in the Order of Service (Mac only).
  • Prevent successful Planning Center account linking from showing the Proclaim homepage.
  • Make sure the sign-in screen can always be moved around the screen.
